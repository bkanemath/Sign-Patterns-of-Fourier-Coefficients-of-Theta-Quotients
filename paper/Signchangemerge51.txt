KOUSTAV BANERJEE, KATHRIN BRINGMANN, BERNHARD HEIM, AND BEN KANE
Abstract. Motivated by conjectures of Schlosser and Zhou on sign patterns of Fourier coeﬃcients
of inﬁnite products, we study two families of theta quotients and their positive integral powers.
We determine asymptotic formulas for their Fourier coeﬃcients and use them to obtain explicit
eventual periodic sign patterns. Our results provide new instances of the sign phenomena observed
by Schlosser and Zhou and illustrate how dominant cusps determine the resulting sign patterns.

6. Open questions
The results of this paper suggest several directions for future research.
(1) It would be interesting to extend Theorems 1.1, 1.3, and 1.5, by considering theta quotients of
the form (for a,b,m,𝓁 ∈ N with a⁄=b)
ϑ (aτ;mτ)𝓁
ϑ (bτ;mτ)𝓁.
For which choices of a,b,m , and 𝓁 do the Fourier coeﬃcients have an eventual periodic sign
pattern? Can this sign pattern be determined explicitly? More generally, can one characterize
the dominant cusps in terms of a,b, and m?
(2) In Theorem 1.5, we assume gcd( m, 6) = 1. Can this assumption be removed? More precisely,
how do the dominant cusps and the resulting sign pattern change if 2 |m or 3|m?
(3) Our results raise a more general question: under what conditions do the Fourier coeﬃcients of
a modular theta quotient have an eventually periodic sign pattern?
